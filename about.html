About Aurelia

A private studio of advisors for people who want a home that feels collected — not merely acquired.

Our story

Eighteen years of quiet representation.

Aurelia began in 2008 as a Greenwich atelier. We grew by referral because we refused to treat homes as inventory. Today we work across Connecticut and New York, still with a small team and a long memory for how our clients actually live.

Our experience spans family estates, city penthouses, waterfront villas, and the in-between houses that never photograph as well as they feel in person — which is why we insist on walking them with you.

Mission

Match people to architecture with honesty.

We exist to shorten the distance between how you want to live and the house that can hold that life.

Vision

Remain the most trusted private brokerage in the corridor.

Known not for volume, but for composure, design literacy, and results that still feel right a decade later.

Values

Discretion. Craft. Clarity.

We speak plainly about condition and price. We protect privacy. We never decorate the truth.

Why choose us

Experience you can feel in the process.

Neighborhood fluency

Advisors who know school calendars, ferry times, and which streets stay quiet after dusk.

Design literacy

We read plans, materials, and light the way a good architect would.

Aftercare

Introductions to makers, gardeners, and movers — only if you want them.
