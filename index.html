Guidance

Questions, answered plainly.

How can I find my dream home?

Begin with a conversation about how you live. We translate that into a private shortlist and arrange viewings only when a home is a genuine match.

Can I schedule a property visit?

Yes. Weekday twilight tours and weekend appointments are available. Use Contact Us or the inquiry form on any listing.

How do I contact a real estate expert?

Call +1 (203) 555-0148, write to hello@aureliaestates.com, or send a note through the form. An advisor replies within one business day.

What services do you provide?

Buying, selling, luxury representation, family home search, and consultation for relocations and portfolios.

How does the property buying process work?

Discovery, shortlist, viewings, offer strategy, diligence, and closing. You receive a written timeline after the first meeting.

Do you work with off-market homes?

Yes. A portion of Aurelia’s collection is shared privately with qualified clients before public release.

How do you price a home for sale?

We combine recent comps, architectural quality, and buyer appetite — then agree a strategy with you before anything is listed.
